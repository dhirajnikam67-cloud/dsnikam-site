The Banking and Storage Rules Tracker

In India the value of banked energy and of a co-located battery is set by rules, not prices.

D. S. Nikam — Independent research, energy storage and power marketsEarly list open · All reports

The rules change by order, amendment and draft, in several states at once. Most of that change never reaches a model until it reaches a bill.

What it will be

Every change that moves a number in the India Renewable Banking Index, within days of the order, with its effect in rupees per kWh on the rows it touches.

The storage position in each state — which regime a co-located battery falls under, what is in force, what is only in draft, and what is missing.

The next ninety days — tariff orders, surcharge petitions, draft notifications and court listings that are due, and what each would move.

Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh to begin with; Maharashtra as its litigation resolves.

What stays free

The reports and their index tables. Nothing already published moves behind a wall.

Who it is for

Open-access and captive developers, battery developers, lenders and their engineers, and large consumers who bank.
