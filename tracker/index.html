Three sample entries

Three positions that are easy to miss when only the headline rule is read. Each entry states where the rule stands today, whether it is in force or only in draft, the clause it rests on, and the date it was last checked against the primary text.

Tamil Nadu · StorageNot provided for

A co-located battery has no deviation category

For deviation settlement, Regulation 13(2) sends solar, wind and wind-solar hybrid generators to the 2024 forecasting regulations, other green generators to the 2019 deviation regulations, and hydro, including pumped storage, outside deviation settlement. A co-located battery is named in none of the three. The same Regulations make storage "Green Energy" when it uses renewable energy, but define "Banking" only for energy from wind, solar and wind-solar hybrid stations.

Why it matters
Which deviation regime applies to a battery behind a solar or wind connection, and whether its stored energy can be banked, is not settled by the text. Until the Commission says so, it rests on contract wording.

Karnataka · Connectivity and GNADraft · date passed

The commencement date has passed. The rules have not.

The draft Connectivity and General Network Access Regulations proposed to come into force on 1 October 2026, repealing the Open Access Regulations, 2025 from the date of notification. No final notification had been located as at 15 October 2026. Until one issues, the draft has no force and the 2025 Regulations continue to apply.

Why it matters
A schedule, guarantee or model that took 1 October 2026 as the start date is running on rules that are not yet in force. Every period that runs from commencement has not begun.

India · CEA · StorageDraft

Two hours of storage, with no end date

Ground-mounted solar and onshore wind plants commissioned after 1 July 2027 are to carry storage of at least 10% of installed capacity for two hours; for plants commissioned after 1 July 2029 and up to 30 June 2031, four hours. The two-hour proviso carries no end date. Read as drafted, the two overlap from 1 July 2029, and after 30 June 2031 only the two-hour proviso is left.

Why it matters
A plant due after mid-2031 cannot tell from the draft whether to plan for two hours of storage or four. The final text of this clause decides it.
