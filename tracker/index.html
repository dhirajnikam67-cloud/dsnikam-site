The Banking and Storage Rules Tracker

In India the value of banked energy and of a co-located battery is set by rules, not prices.

The rules change by order, amendment and draft, in several states at once. Most of that change never reaches a model until it reaches a bill.

What it will be

Every change that moves a number in the India Renewable Banking Index, within days of the order, with its effect in rupees per kWh on the rows it touches.

The storage position in each state — which regime a co-located battery falls under, what is in force, what is only in draft, and what is missing.

The next ninety days — tariff orders, surcharge petitions, draft notifications and court listings that are due, and what each would move.

Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Gujarat and Andhra Pradesh to begin with; Maharashtra as its litigation resolves.

Three sample entries

Each entry states where a rule stands today, whether it is in force or only in draft, the clause it rests on, and the date it was last checked against the primary text.

Gujarat · BankingIn force

Banking charge for green energy open access

Rs 1.00 per unit from 1 September 2026 to 31 March 2027. From 1 April 2027 the charge is to be determined under Regulation 17.6.

India · CEA · StorageDraft

Co-located storage with new solar and wind plants

Ground-mounted solar and onshore wind plants commissioned after 1 July 2027 to carry storage of at least 10% of installed capacity for two hours; for plants commissioned after 1 July 2029 and up to 30 June 2031, four hours. The two-hour proviso carries no end date.

Rests on
Draft CEA (Technical Standards for Construction of Electric Plants and Electric Lines) 2nd Amendment Regulations, 2026, proposed Regulation 106B(20)
Published
Draft dated 03.09.2026; comments closed 15.10.2026 (extended from 04.10.2026)
Checked
15.10.2026
Tamil Nadu · BankingIn force

Who can bank green energy, and on what terms

No banking for third-party purchase or sale. Banked energy must be used within the same calendar-month billing cycle, with no carry-forward, at a banking charge of 8% in kind. Wind plants commissioned on or before 31 March 2018 keep twelve-month banking, April to March, at 14% in kind.

Rests on
TNERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) Regulations, 2025, Regulation 16(1), 16(4) and 16(5)
Published
Notification No. TNERC/GEOA/25-1/2025, dated 18.09.2025
Checked
15.10.2026
