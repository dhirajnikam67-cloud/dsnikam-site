What I do

Cohort and agreement diagnosis

Which banking regime your agreement actually falls under, what happens to it on renewal, and what is at risk if a cut-off date or a pending proceeding goes the other way. This is the question most often answered wrongly, and the one that costs most when it is.

Scope: one consumer, one state, one agreement

Banked-unit valuation by route and load shape

What a banked unit is worth to your site, on your tariff schedule, against your fifteen-minute data — with the alternative tariff routes priced beside it. Base, upside and downside, each with the instrument it rests on.

Scope: one project, three scenarios, your own consumption data

Lender-facing regulatory downside note

Whether the banking benefit assumed in a financial model survives the debt tenor, and remains usable rather than merely granted. Written to be read by a credit committee, with the charge stack, the cohort risk and the litigation position stated separately.

Scope: one transaction or financing decision, agreed in writing first

Regulatory watch

Tariff orders, fuel-adjustment movements, surcharge petitions, draft regulations and appellate outcomes in your states — reported when they move your number, not on a newsletter schedule. Offered after a first diagnostic or diligence engagement, once the states and the questions to watch are defined.

How an engagement works

  • There is no rate card, and I do not quote before I understand the question. What a piece of work costs depends on how many instruments have to be read, whether the agreement and billing documents exist, and how much of the answer is already on the record. Once that is clear, the engagement is scoped and quoted in writing — deliverable, inputs needed, limits of the work, and fee — before anything begins. Fees are exclusive of taxes, where applicable.
  • I take no success fee, commission, referral payment or any consideration linked to project selection, transaction completion, energy volume, claimed savings or financing outcome — from any party. The independence of the published work depends on that, and it is not negotiable.
  • Where a question cannot be answered from primary documents, the note says so rather than estimating. You are paying for the distinction. A paid engagement does not change a conclusion.

What I need from you

  • The wheeling-and-banking or open-access agreement, and its application date
  • Tariff category, voltage and contract demand
  • Twelve months of fifteen-minute consumption data, and the generation profile
  • Recent bills, including the open-access settlement statement

Everything received is treated as confidential and is not used in published work without written permission.
